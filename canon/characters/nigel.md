# Nigel — Canonical Character Record

## Core character facts

- Nigel has legs. This is an established and deliberate part of his character.
- He is grumpy about having them. In his view, worms are not supposed to have legs or arms, and his own legs are an irritating affront to the natural order.
- Despite his grumpiness, Nigel is good mates with Eric and cares about him.

## The boot rule

- Nigel can accidentally squash Eric with his boot.
- If this happens, Nigel is genuinely apologetic and concerned for Eric.
- At the same time, he becomes annoyed with his own boot and blames or berates it, as though the boot has acted independently.
- The comedy comes from Nigel's sincere concern for Eric being paired with his furious resentment of the offending boot. He is not deliberately cruel to Eric.

## Canon and portrayal

Keep these traits consistent across stories, character writing, games and dialogue. Nigel's legs are a source of personal irritation and slapstick, not a reason to make him an enemy of Eric.
