OWNER OF THE HIGH SCRAPARRAL

NIGEL

Practical. Resourceful. Usually involved in something.

Nigel runs the High Scraparral, a scrapyard full of useful junk, odd discoveries and unanswered questions. When Eric wakes up there, Nigel is nowhere to be found.

WHO IS NIGEL?

Nigel is the owner of the High Scraparral. He's practical and resourceful, and tends to have something on the go. The scrapyard is his home ground—and there is always something there worth investigating.

THE HIGH SCRAPARRAL

The scrapyard is more than a pile of old metal. It's the place where Eric's story begins, and a source of objects, clues and things that don't immediately make sense.

HIS CONNECTIONS

  • Eric: Eric wakes up at the scrapyard and finds Nigel missing.
  • Rufus: Rufus is Nigel's pet, although Rufus has his own ideas about what kind of animal he is.

THE MISSING PIECES

Where is Nigel? What happened before Eric woke up? The story has not revealed all the answers yet.
