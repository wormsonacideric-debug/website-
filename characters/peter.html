ONE OF THE EARLY BIRDS

PETER

Fast. Frightening. Always swooping into Eric’s day.

Peter is an Early Bird — and from Eric’s point of view, a rather alarming one. When Peter takes to the air, Eric has to keep moving and watch out for those swoops.

WHO IS PETER?

Peter belongs to the Early Birds, the flock that turns a quiet moment into a scramble for survival. He is one of the characters who makes Eric’s world feel considerably less safe.

THE LOOK

Peter’s canonical design features enormous bloodshot yellow eyes, ragged grey feathers, an iridescent green-and-purple neck, pale grey wings with dark markings, a dark crumbed beak, red feet and black claws.

IN THE GAME

In Peter’s Peril, Peter swoops and banks through the action while Eric tries to dodge the attacks and survive long enough to collect the fallen pink donuts.

Do not assume the sky is safe. Peter is an Early Bird, and Eric has every reason to keep an eye on him.
