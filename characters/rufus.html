A SHARK WITH A DOG'S SELF-IMAGE

RUFUS

Loyal. Hungry. Complicated. Absolutely convinced he's a dog.

Rufus is a shark, Nigel's pet, and a character whose understanding of himself doesn't quite match reality. He's loyal, he's hungry, and the gap between what he is and what he thinks he is is part of the fun.

SHARK. DOG. RUFUS.

Rufus is a shark who believes he is a dog. The world may have questions about this arrangement. Rufus appears to have made up his mind.

HIS HUMAN—WELL, WORM—CONNECTION

Rufus is Nigel's pet. That connection ties him to the High Scraparral and to the mystery of Nigel's absence.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Loyalty, in Rufus's own way.
  • A healthy appetite.
  • A complicated relationship with the idea of being a dog.

MORE TO COME

Rufus has plenty of room to get into trouble as Eric's world expands. His story will grow here as more of it is revealed.
