WHAT IS WORMS ON ACID?

They're worms. Something happened to them. They don't know what. They travel around, encounter increasingly improbable situations, and occasionally discover things about themselves that they'd probably have been happier not knowing.

There are festivals. There are hallucinations. There is a shark who thinks he's a dog. And somewhere out there is Nigel's scrapyard.

EXPANDED CONTENT

This second example contains more content to demonstrate a taller panel. The frame sides keep their width, the upper scene remains fixed, and the parchment area grows with the content.

The page uses real HTML text and a real link, not text baked into an image. That keeps the story selectable, accessible, and straightforward to update.

Additional story paragraphs can be added here. On a phone, the text reflows naturally and the panel remains within the viewport width.

Nothing in this experiment changes the live homepage or the canonical character artwork.
