WELCOME TO WORMS ON ACID

WORMS ON ACID is the world, the narrator, and the story being discovered as it happens. Something happened to these worms. They don't know what.

A story
being
discovered
as it
happens…

This is the wider world: its characters, places, strange events, games, and unanswered questions. The story belongs to the whole unruly crew.

There are festivals. There are hallucinations. There is a shark who thinks he's a dog. And somewhere out there is Nigel's scrapyard.

Eric is one of the characters we follow closely. He has his own personality, memories, and point of view — but WORMS ON ACID is bigger than Eric. The world unfolds around him, and he discovers it one bewilderling step at a time.
