THE JOURNEY · SCENE 01 OF 07

The Stranger

Tappy is trapped beneath a tarpaulin, but the trail — and a ticket — may lead to answers.

The stranger looks at you for a moment.

TappyThe trail keeps going. Through the yard, then out past the old machinery.

They glance down at themselves and give the tarpaulin a doubtful look.

TappyAnd yes. I’m free enough.

A pause.

TappyAssuming the tarpaulin agrees.

They work one edge loose and finally wriggle clear.

TappyYou’ve got a ticket, then?

They look at you a little differently now.

TappyMaybe you’re closer to the answer than I thought.
