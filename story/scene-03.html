It’s Tappy

The stranger’s sleepy voice becomes familiar. Eric discovers who has been under the tarpaulin.

The worm gives you a sleepy look.

TappyEric…

You recognise the voice. It’s Tappy.

He finally wriggles free of the tarpaulin and looks back towards the trail.

TappyI was wondering when you were going to wake me up.

He looks at you for a moment.

TappyAnd before you ask… no, I don't remember much either.

A pause.

TappyBut I remember there was music.
