THE JOURNEY · SCENE 07 OF 07

The Warehouse, Manchester, 1994

Tappy recognises the venue and date on the ticket: The Warehouse, Manchester, Saturday 23 September 1994.

Tappy leans in as Eric nudges the ticket around.

TappyHang on…

He squints at it.

TappyThat’s The Warehouse.

A pause.

TappyManchester.

He looks at Eric, suddenly less certain.

TappyI know that name.

He looks back at the ticket.

TappySaturday… 23 September ’94.

Then, quieter:

TappyAnd there’s music on it.

EricThe Warehouse. Manchester. 1994. I nudge the ticket flat. That’s a bit further back than I expected. And music on it? Is that the kind that makes a place feel real, or the kind that makes you wonder if the place made you? I look at Tappy. If you know the name, don’t hold back just because the scrap yard’s listening.
