THE FESTIVALS

Music. Lights. Worms. Things get weird.

THE PULL OF THE FESTIVAL

Festivals mean music, crowds, colour, lights and the promise that something might happen. For Eric and the crew, they are another part of a world that keeps getting stranger.

They're places to get lost, find something unexpected, and make decisions that may look different in the morning.

GETTING IN IS HALF THE TROUBLE

Eric has a talent for finding himself on the wrong side of a sensible plan. Getting into a festival without paying is one such problem—and security has a habit of making problems more complicated.

There may be gates, patrols, hiding places and opportunities to make a perfectly ordinary situation much worse.

WHAT HAPPENS THERE?

Music, misadventures and the occasional missing piece of memory. The festivals are part of the larger journey, not a neat little detour with everything explained at the end.

THE MORNING AFTER

Sometimes the story of a night is easier to tell than the night itself.

We won't pretend every detail is known yet. The festivals will reveal themselves as Eric gets there.
